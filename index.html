To Jewel,

I've been holding onto this for a very long time. I tried naman na pigilan pero ewan ko why does it keeps getting deeper lang. The more I try na di magka gusto sayo, mas lalong lumalalim yung nararamdaman ko. So i guess this is it.

The moment I saw you, you already caught my attention na talaga. At first it was just silly crush, "happy crush" kumbaga. Then I started to make a move intentionally just to get a glimpse of you even for jus a few seconds and then we got closer tapos wala na katapusan ko na. Kaya nga iniiwasan kong maging ka close ka kasi I know to myself na I can't stop once I got closer to you and I did.

Natatakot akong mag sabi sayo kahit na super obvious naman na kasi I'm afraid na baka may magbago satin, sa bond natin and the way we interact sa isa't isa. I know na possible yung mga changes pero ayoko kasi eh since you're really special para sakin to the point na I can't even afford to lose you at all. So kahit sobran halata nako, mas pinipili kong wag nalang umamin rather than lose something precious to me.

I don't know why, how and when did it all started na lumalim pero ang alam ko lang na reason bakit kita nagugustuhan up until now is not just because maganda ka or what, pero kasi ikaw na yan. I don't think I'll ever need bunch of reasons bakit. Kasi alam ko namang as time goes by, those reasons will eventually disappear. So hindi kita ginusto for a reason, ginusto kita kasi ikaw na yan. Yung iba't ibang possible reason dumadagdag lang naman bawat araw eh.

Sobrang dami ko pang gustong sabihin honestly pero hindi ko alam bakit hindi ko sya ma form in to words so maybe I'll leave it nalang. I hope we don't make things awkward after this.
